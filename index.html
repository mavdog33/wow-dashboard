Bank / hold — 4pc & hit rules (equip logic)

The set rule: you own all 5 Beast Lord pieces but only need 4. Your one "open" set slot is always whichever piece has the best non-set upgrade — that's Leggings (→ T4 legs / Skulker's). Helm/Mantle/Cuirass/Handguards are permanent because their alternatives (T4 helm, Beastmaw, T4 chest, Liar's Tongue) are all worse. That's why T4 helm/shoulders/chest bank while T4 legs equips. T4 chest also can't co-exist with T4 legs (would drop you to 3 BL pieces) — bank it for the SSC/TK era when the whole set dissolves.
